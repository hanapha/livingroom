Decorative wall treatments introduce subtle architectural interest while enhancing the visual depth of living room interiors beautifully. Living room spaces should feel comfortable for both entertaining guests and enjoying quiet moments alone.

Accent decor pieces should complement the overall living room style while adding personality and visual interest. Modern living room spaces often blend minimalism and warmth to create interiors that feel both stylish and highly livable.
